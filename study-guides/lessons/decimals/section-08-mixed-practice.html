8.1

Choosing the Operation

Goal: I can choose +, −, ×, or ÷ from the story. Mixes core (+ −) and supplemental (× ÷) problems.
Show how

Gabriella has $10.00. She buys a book for $6.45. How much money does she have left?

  1. Find the question: “How much … left?” — something is taken away.
  2. Choose the operation with the clue words below: “left” means subtract.
  3. Estimate: 10 − 6 = about $4. Solve: 10.00 − 6.45 = 3.55.
  4. Check and label: 3.55 + 6.45 = 10.00 ✓. She has $3.55 left.
Addin all, total, altogether, combined, sum, how much … together
Subtractleft, remain, how much more / less, how much taller / longer, difference, change
Multiplyeach (and you want the total), per, equal groups of, times as much, area
Divideshare equally, split, each (and you know the total), how many … fit, per
Why it works: the operation comes from the story, not the numbers. Ask: am I joining, taking away, making equal groups, or splitting into equal groups?
