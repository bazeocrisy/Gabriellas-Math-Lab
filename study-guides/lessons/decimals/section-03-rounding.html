3.1

Rounding on a Number Line

Goal: I can use a number line to find the closer whole number.
Show how

Round 2.7 to the nearest whole number.

  1. 2.7 is between the whole numbers 2 and 3. Put them at the ends of the line.
  2. Find the halfway point: 2.5 (the tall middle tick).
  3. 2.7 is past halfway, so it is closer to 3. 2.7 rounds to 3.
  4. Exactly halfway? 2.5 rounds up to 3. That is the rule everyone uses.
Why it works: rounding means “which number is closer?” The halfway point splits the line: left half goes down, right half (and halfway) goes up.

Rounding to the Nearest Whole Number

Goal: I can round using the rounding digit and the digit to its right.
Show how

Round 4.735 to the nearest whole number.

  1. Find the rounding digit. Nearest whole number means the ones place: the 4.
  2. Look at the digit to its right: the 7 in the tenths place.
  3. 5 or more? Round up. 7 is 5 or more, so the 4 goes up to 5. (0 to 4? Keep the rounding digit.)
  4. Drop the digits after the rounding place: 4.735 → 5
Why it works: the digit to the right tells you which half you are in. 4.7 is past the halfway point 4.5, so 4.735 is closer to 5. Only that one digit matters.

Rounding to the Nearest Tenth

Goal: I can round a decimal to the nearest tenth.
Show how

Round 4.76 to the nearest tenth.

  1. Rounding digit: the tenths digit, 7. So 4.76 is between 4.7 and 4.8.
  2. Digit to its right: the hundredths digit, 6. 6 is 5 or more, so round up: 7 becomes 8.
  3. Drop the digits after the tenths place: 4.76 → 4.8. The number line agrees: 4.76 is past halfway (4.75).
  4. Rounding up a 9? Regroup, like adding 1: 2.96 → 2.9 + 0.1 = 3.0.
Why it works: it is the same rule as rounding to a whole number. Only the rounding place changes.

Rounding to the Nearest Hundredth

Goal: I can round a decimal to the nearest hundredth.
Show how

Round 4.735 to the nearest hundredth.

  1. Rounding digit: the hundredths digit, 3. So 4.735 is between 4.73 and 4.74.
  2. Digit to its right: the thousandths digit, 5. 5 means round up: 3 becomes 4.
  3. Drop the digit after the hundredths place: 4.735 → 4.74. On the line, 4.735 is exactly halfway, and halfway rounds up.
Why it works: same two questions every time: Where is the rounding digit? and Is the next digit 5 or more? Keep the zeros you need: 3.995 → 4.00.

3.5

Rounding Word Problems

Goal: I can find the number and the rounding place in a story, then round.
Show how

Maya ran 3.68 miles. About how far did she run, to the nearest tenth of a mile?

  1. Find the number: 3.68. Find the rounding place: “nearest tenth” → the tenths digit, 6.
  2. Digit to its right: 8. 8 is 5 or more, so round up: 6 becomes 7.
  3. Answer with units: Maya ran about 3.7 miles.
Clue words: “about”, “nearly”, “estimate”, “to the nearest …”. Money to the nearest dollar = nearest whole number; to the nearest dime = nearest tenth; to the nearest cent = nearest hundredth.
